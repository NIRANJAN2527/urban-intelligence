AUTONOMOUS SENSING INFRASTRUCTURE

Continuous city-wide monitoring without dedicated surveillance fleets.

01

Mobile Transit Fleet

Public municipal buses cover hundreds of kilometers of city roads daily. By equipping existing transit vehicles with edge-sensing hardware, the entire municipal grid achieves continuous real-time coverage.

02

Real-Time Edge Vision

Onboard neural networks process road surfaces at high framerates. Spatial correlation algorithms filter transient vibrations, verify defect signatures, and correlate detections with precise high-accuracy GPS telemetry.

03

Automated Governance

Verified anomalies are categorized by municipal jurisdiction and prioritized by risk score. Road defects, traffic bottlenecks, and public safety events are routed straight to municipal action teams.
